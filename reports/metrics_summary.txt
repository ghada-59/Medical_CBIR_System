INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Precision@1 measures whether the first retrieved image belongs to the same BUSI class as the query.
Precision@5 measures the proportion of the five retrieved images that belong to the same BUSI class as the query.
These metrics evaluate image retrieval consistency, not clinical diagnosis or cancer classification.

Note: Results are based on classical image descriptors and Euclidean similarity and are intended for academic CBIR evaluation.
